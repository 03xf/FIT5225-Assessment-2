# Individual Report Template (maximum 500 words)

## Role and contribution (~150 words)

State your personal responsibility, concrete deliverables, commits/PRs, technical decisions and how your work contributed to the delivered multi-cloud system. Use your own words and only claim work you personally performed.

## Teamwork reflection (~150 words)

Reflect independently on collaboration, feedback, challenges, handovers and what the team did well or should improve. Do not coordinate this wording with team members.

## Generative AI declaration

State precisely whether and how GenAI tools were used. Confirm that you reviewed the resulting work and can explain or modify it. This declaration is required by the assignment even if GenAI was not used.
